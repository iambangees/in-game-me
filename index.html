Ready to run?

Your photo becomes the runner's face. No photo? You run with the default face — the game is the same either way.

Put yourself in the game

Your photo is cropped to a circle and drawn as the runner's face. It never leaves your browser — it is stored only on this device, and you can remove it any time.

How to play

← → or swipe — switch lane  ·  ↑ / Space or swipe up — jump
↓ or swipe down — slide  ·  P — pause
🟧 Orange barrier — jump it  ·  🟪 Purple bar — slide under it
🚂 Red train — can't jump or slide it, switch lane  ·  🪙 — 5 points each

The pace starts slow and builds gently, and a new stage starts every 250 m. You also carry a 🛡 shield: the first thing you hit each run breaks the shield instead of ending the game (watch for the cyan ring around the runner) — the second hit is the wipeout. Tap ⛶ Full screen for the full wide-screen view. Best score is saved on this device only.

Honest limits: this is a 2D runner — your photo becomes the character's face on a drawn runner, not a full 3D avatar of you. Everything runs in your browser; no account, no uploads, no ads.
